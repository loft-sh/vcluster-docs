A connector configures a platform integration, such as a shared database, Argo CD, or observability. Connectors are cluster-scoped. Callers who can update a connector see its full spec. All other callers, including connector viewers and tenants, see a redacted view without credentials or connection details. Tenants see only the connectors assigned to them and cannot change them. Watch isn't supported.

## Connector example

An example Connector:
```yaml
apiVersion: management.loft.sh/v1
kind: Connector
metadata:
  name: shared-postgres
spec:
  displayName: Shared PostgreSQL
  sharedDatabase:
    dialect: postgres
    endpoint: postgres.example.com
    password: replace-with-database-password
    port: "5432"
    user: platform
status: {}

```
