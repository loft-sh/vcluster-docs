:::warning Platform-managed namespaces are deprecated
Namespaces managed through projects, also known as spaces, are deprecated in v5.0.0.
Platform management of namespaces is planned for removal in a later v5.0 patch release.
Existing Kubernetes namespaces and their workloads will remain on their clusters after removal. The platform will no longer manage them.

Until then, platform-managed namespaces keep working in projects outside tenants. Tenant projects can't create or import them, and they can't be moved into a tenant project.
Move workloads that need continued platform management to [clusters](../../use-platform/clusters/create/create-with-template.mdx).
:::
