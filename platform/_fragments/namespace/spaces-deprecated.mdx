:::warning Namespaces are deprecated
Namespaces (spaces) are deprecated in v5.0.0 and are planned for removal in a later v5.0 patch release. They keep working in projects outside tenants, and the UI shows a deprecation banner on the namespace pages. Tenant projects can't use namespaces. Move workloads to [clusters](../../use-platform/clusters/create/create-with-template.mdx).
:::
