This section covers running vCluster Platform itself. It's usually work for the
system admin who installed the platform. The changes made here affect how the
platform runs for everyone, rather than what one tenant or project can do.

The platform is the management plane for your clusters. It runs as the
`vcluster-platform` Helm chart and is the source of truth for every platform
decision. Admins and automation reach it through the UI, CLI, and API. Its
controllers reconcile what they define into connected control plane clusters.
On each of those clusters, an agent keeps an outbound connection to the
platform, and cluster control planes run there.

By default, the platform runs as a single replica with an embedded database.
Most of this section covers what you change from there, such as who can sign
in, which clusters the platform manages, how it survives failures, and how you
keep it current. For the components in detail, see
[Architecture](../overview/architecture.mdx) and
[How Platform works](../overview/how-platform-works.mdx).

## Work in this section

The groups follow the platform's life. You configure it and decide who can sign
in, connect the clusters it manages, and make it reliable. Then you watch it,
keep it current, and fix what goes wrong.
