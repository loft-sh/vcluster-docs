When vCluster Labs needs access to your infrastructure, the connection originates from the IPv4 addresses below. Allowlist them on your firewall so the connection gets through.

```text title="Europe (Frankfurt)"
63.183.170.86/32
18.196.66.114/32
```

```text title="US East (Ohio)"
3.19.214.126/32
18.227.187.66/32
```

Allowlist all four addresses, or only one region's pair if vCluster Labs tells you which region connects to you. A connection can originate from either address in a region.

Allowlisting an address doesn't grant access on its own. Limit the allowed protocols and ports to the ones the connection needs.
