description: Stable public IPv4 addresses that vCluster Labs services use when they connect to customer infrastructure that requires a fixed source address.
---

When a vCluster Labs service needs to reach your infrastructure from a fixed source address, its connections originate from the stable IPv4 addresses below. Only destinations agreed with you during onboarding use these addresses. The connection can use any protocol the service needs, such as SSH, HTTPS, or the Kubernetes API.

| Region | Availability zone | IP address |
|---|---|---|
| Europe (Frankfurt) | `eu-central-1a` | `63.183.170.86` |
| Europe (Frankfurt) | `eu-central-1b` | `18.196.66.114` |
| US East (Ohio) | `us-east-2a` | `3.19.214.126` |
| US East (Ohio) | `us-east-2b` | `18.227.187.66` |

## Allowlist the addresses

Allowlist both addresses in the region assigned to your service. A connection can originate from either one, for example after an availability zone failure.

```text title="Europe (Frankfurt)"
63.183.170.86/32
18.196.66.114/32
```

```text title="US East (Ohio)"
3.19.214.126/32
18.227.187.66/32
```

Before moving a service to the other region, vCluster Labs asks you to allowlist that region's addresses.

Allowlisting an address doesn't grant access on its own. Restrict the allowed protocols and ports on your firewall to the ones agreed for the service.
