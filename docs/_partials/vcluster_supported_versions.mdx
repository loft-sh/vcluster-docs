Due to rapid development, each minor version receives active support for 3 months after its release. Active support ends on the End of Support (EOS) date. For 3 months after the EOS date, the version receives only critical security updates. After the End of Life (EOL) date, the version receives no updates. During active support, updates from the active development branch can be backported upon request.

Long Term Support (LTS) releases have longer support windows. See [LTS releases](#lts-releases).

Versions not on the following list are no longer in any support window.

## LTS releases

vCluster v0.37 and vCluster Platform v4.12 are designated Long Term Support (LTS) releases. LTS support covers the two releases as a pair, because the platform and the clusters it manages are built and tested together. To stay on LTS, run vCluster v0.37 for your clusters on vCluster Platform v4.12.

| Phase | Standard release | LTS release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active support | 3 months | 12 months |
| End of Support (EOS) period | 3 months | 6 months |

During active support, you can request backports of fixes from the active development branch, including support for newer Kubernetes versions. During the EOS period, LTS releases receive only critical security updates.

The LTS dates for v0.37 apply only when vCluster v0.37 runs with vCluster Platform v4.12. Without the platform, vCluster v0.37 follows the standard lifecycle, with EOS on 2026 Dec 08 and EOL on 2027 Mar 08. Upgrading either product past its LTS release takes you off LTS. See the [Platform lifecycle policy](/docs/platform/maintenance/upgrade-migrate/lifecycle-policy) for Platform dates.
