Due to the rapid development of the platform, each minor version receives active support for 6 months after its release. Active support ends on the End of Support (EOS) date. For 3 months after the EOS date, the version receives only critical security updates. After the End of Life (EOL) date, the version receives no updates. During active support, updates from the active development branch can be backported upon request.

Long Term Support (LTS) releases have longer support windows. See [LTS releases](#lts-releases).

Versions not on the following list are no longer in any support window.

## LTS releases

vCluster Platform v4.12 and vCluster v0.37 are designated Long Term Support (LTS) releases. LTS support covers the two releases as a pair, because the platform and the clusters it manages are built and tested together. To stay on LTS, run vCluster Platform v4.12 with vCluster v0.37 for your clusters. vMetal is part of vCluster Platform, so vMetal in v4.12 has the same LTS dates.

| Phase | Standard release | LTS release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active support | 6 months | 12 months |
| End of Support (EOS) period | 3 months | 6 months |

During active support, you can request backports of fixes from the active development branch, including support for newer Kubernetes versions. During the EOS period, LTS releases receive only critical security updates.

Clusters that run a vCluster version other than v0.37 follow the standard lifecycle for that version. Upgrading either product past its LTS release takes you off LTS. See the [vCluster lifecycle policy](/docs/vcluster/manage/upgrade/supported_versions) for vCluster dates.
