### Control plane options

Decide the various options of how you want your control plane deployed:

- **[High availability](../../../deploy/control-plane/kubernetes-pod/high-availability.mdx)** - Run multiple copies of vCluster components.
- **[Rootless mode](../../../deploy/control-plane/kubernetes-pod/security/rootless-mode.mdx)** - Deploy the vCluster pod without root access to the control plane cluster.
- **Backing Store** - Decide how the data of your cluster is stored.
